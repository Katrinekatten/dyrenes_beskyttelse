Before you post

Before you post make sure....

  • Is the information correct?

  • Does the visual follow our colors & typography?

  • Is the animal represented respectfully?

  • Is the message short and easy to understand?

  • Does the post have one clear purpose?

  • Is there a clear CTA when needed?

  • Does it sound like Roskilde Internat?
