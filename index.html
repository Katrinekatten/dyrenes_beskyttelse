The purpose

Social media guide

This guide is designed to make the social media plan easier, clearer and more consistent for Roskilde Internat.
It brings together a clear social media strategy and a practical content plan, with simple guidelines, ready-to-use templates and tools to help plan and create content.
The goal is simple: stay consistent while keeping the content real, human and connected to the daily life of the shelter.

Content stradgedy

Create a recognizable and engaging social media presence that brings people closer to Roskilde Internat, gives every animal visibility and turns followers into adopters, supporters and an active local community.

  • Build a community : Create a recognizable account people want to follow and come back to.

  • Find the right home : Give animals regular visibility and help potential adopters understand who they are and what they need.

  • Turn attention into action : Use content to encourage meaningful actions: visit the website, adopt, donate, share or get involved.
