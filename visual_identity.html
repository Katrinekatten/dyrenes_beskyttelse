DO'S

  • Real animals from Roskilde Internat

  • Clear and good quality pictures

  • Real shelter environment

  • Candid moments

  • Show personality and behaviour

DON'TS

  • Costumes

  • Humanising animals

  • Artificial staging

  • Excessive filters

  • Overly sad / dramatic editing

  • Too much text covering the animal
